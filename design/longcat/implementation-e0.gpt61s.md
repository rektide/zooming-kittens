## Discovered failures and corrections

### A retired attempt still owes geometry quietness

Real topology/focus interaction switched focus while an owned neutral result was
quieting. The old attempt correctly retired and drained, but the group was already
acquired and non-yielding. `next_reconsideration` filtered out its future quiet
deadline once no current attempt remained. Stock dedup then supplied no further
event, leaving an `Acquiring/Quiet` wait indefinitely.

The deterministic pure regression first failed with **`None` instead of
`Some(1010)`** after focus retirement at 520ms and last owned geometry at 510ms.
The fix exposes each eligible group's **future** quiet deadline. The existing
strict `at > now` filter prevents expired-timer spins. At that deadline the new
focus gets a fresh Taffy goal; preferences/generation remain unchanged. It grants
no stale command permission and does not bypass active yielding or receipt drain.

### Residual native clamps must not become new preferences

The real recipient-maximum fixture initially responded to a +16px first edge by
keeping the recipient at 180px, subtracting 16px from the donor and compensating
in the residual last row. The requested boundary stayed put while its suffix
shifted backward. Earlier classification treated this as unrelated motion,
accepted new sibling ratios and reacquired more attempts against the same clamp.
The failing run eventually learned a spurious 11px manual floor. That result was
rejected, not adopted as an acceptable limitation.

A real donor minimum produced another narrow signature: `[195,230,187]` became
`[187,230,195]` after a positive useful step. Reasserting the normalized native
floor reversed the transfer, kept the donor stationary and shifted the rigid
suffix, compensating in the last row. A bare reversal is normally learnable;
this **coupled residual pattern while the position response remains unresolved**
needs the more conservative execution classification.

`residual_tail_compensation` recognizes only positive-edge, at-least-three-member
rigid-suffix fingerprints within its stated pixel bounds: stationary recipient
plus a suffix shift matching the attempted donor transfer, or a small reversed
transfer with stationary donor and matching suffix. Existing requested-progress
coupling and incompatible commit-only holds remain intact. These end the attempt
limited, hold its reference, drain the receipt and do not learn or quietly retry.
Both reply-first and position-first regressions pass. Two- **and three-member
isolated reversals** still become unattributed guidance in either reply order.

This is conservative geometry, not a newly reported client bound or proof that a
human did not act. A coincident external edit with the exact clamp fingerprint may
be limited until another independent input. Arbitrary client constraints cannot
be causally distinguished from external motion on this stock surface. No failed
native target was learned merely to make acceptance pass.
